The Saturday Smash tour 🎾

How the app runs our Saturdays today – and a sneak peek at what's coming next.

← Back to the app

How it works now

One link for everything: tinyurl.com/kltc-play – add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

📲

Put it on your phone

iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Install app. It opens full screen with the 🎾 icon and updates itself.

🎴

Make your player card – once

A 2-minute quiz: are you The Wall, The Net Ninja or The Big Hitter? Tap your name from the group list – no typing, no fake names. Change it any time with ✏️ Update my card.

🔒

Your phone gets a ✅ once

The first time you answer from a phone, Jurate confirms it's really you. After that your answers count straight away – nobody can answer in your name.

📅

Monday 9am: "Are you in?"

The app gets the week ready by itself and opens the Monday message with the forecast and kit tip – Jurate presses Send to the group.

Automatic · Jurate sends
👍

Tap in or out – 20 seconds

In, in + 1:30 warm-up, reserve, or out with a reason. No more chasing in the group chat.

🍽️

Order your Saturday menu

Three sessions – 🥗 Starter, 🍝 Main, 🍰 Dessert. Pick mixed, ladies'/men's doubles, singles or chef's choice for each – plus 🔥 forehand or 🎯 backhand side.

📥

Answers arrive by themselves

Every few minutes (7am–11pm) new cards and answers move into the organiser app and onto everyone's phones.

Automatic
☁️

Forecast and kit check

The Saturday forecast and a "what to wear" tip refresh twice a day, up to 2 weeks ahead.

Automatic
🎾

Order of play

Built from who's in, everyone's menu, levels and sides – partners mix every session. Jurate locks it and it's on every phone.

🚪

Can't make it? Drop out

Tap 🚪 Drop out and pick a reason. Only you can take yourself out – once you're in, nobody else can untick you.

🦸

Short of players?

The app shows who's on reserve or on call, with a one-tap WhatsApp to ask them. Whoever steps in gets the 🦸 Hero badge.

Jurate sends
💬

Group chat in the app

Lifts, swaps, banter. Only approved phones can post, so every name is the real person. WhatsApp stays for anything urgent.

🏆

Ladder and cards

Win 3, draw 2, lose 1, hero +2. Collect everyone's player cards in the sticker album – tap one to flip it.

🔐

Private stays private

Levels, priorities, away dates and phone numbers are only in Jurate's organiser app – never on the public page.
